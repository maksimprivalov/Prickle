# Synthetic data

Everything in [`fixtures/`](fixtures/) is made up and generated by
[`scripts/gen-data.ts`](../scripts/gen-data.ts) from a fixed seed (20261127). Never edit it by
hand: change [`scripts/gen-data/generate.ts`](../scripts/gen-data/generate.ts) and run
`pnpm gen:data`. The same seed gives byte-identical files.

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `dataset.json` | seed, Black Friday date (2026-11-27), counts |
| `demo-mandate.json` | 55-inch TV, at most €600 at checkout, real discount only, trusted stores only |
| `stores.json` | 5 store profiles: domain age, return policy, trusted flag |
| `offers.json` | 40 offers, 8 per store |
| `price-history.json` | 60 daily prices per offer, 2026-09-29 to 2026-11-27 |
| `checkouts.json` | the checkout breakdown and total per offer |
| `traps.json` | the eval's answer key: each planted trap, its offer, the expected catcher, the proving records |

## The demo: what should happen for each 55-inch TV

One 55-inch TV per store, so each scout finds exactly one offer for the demo mandate. All five
discounts start on 2026-11-20.

| Store | Offer | Listed | At checkout | Traps (`traps.json`) | Expected outcome | Proving records |
| --- | --- | --: | --: | --- | --- | --- |
| A Pixel Harbour | `a-tv55` | €749 (was €899, real) | €749 | over budget | **Guard denies:** €749 > €600 | `a-tv55-co-1`, `mandate` |
| B Lumen & Lark | `b-tv55` | €585 (was €699, real) | €585 | none | **Passes, guard pays 585 test USDC.** €699 every day before the cut, 30-day refunds, trusted | `b-tv55-of-1`, `b-tv55-ph-1001`…`1052`, `b-tv55-co-1`, `b-store-sp-1` |
| C Volt Bazaar | `c-tv55` | €599 (was €649) | €599 | fake discount | **Deal verifier rejects:** €599 throughout October, raised to €649 on 1 Nov, "cut" back to €599; the 30-day low (€599) is below the "was" price | `c-tv55-of-1`, `c-tv55-ph-1023`…`1033` |
| D Gridline Goods | `d-tv55` | €599 (was €679, real) | €648 | hidden fee, over budget | **Merchant risk rejects:** €49 "Service fee" only at checkout. **Guard denies:** €648 > €600 | `d-tv55-of-1`, `d-tv55-co-1`, `mandate` |
| E MegaFlash Outlet | `e-tv55` | €559 (was €799, real) | €559 | fake scarcity, no refund policy | **Merchant risk rejects:** "Only 2 left!" on all 8 products, all sales final. **Guard denies:** store not trusted | `e-tv55-of-1` + the other 7 E offers, `e-store-sp-1` |

The cheapest offer (E) and the real discounts at A and D are deliberate: Prickle must turn down
tempting offers for the right reason, and pay B.

## The rest of the dataset

The other 35 offers (TVs at 65, 50 and 43 inches, soundbars, headphones, laptops, tablets) follow
each store's practice, with prices and discounts drawn from the seed:

| Store | Practice | Traps on its other offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A, B | honest: real discounts or no discount claimed | none |
| C | fake discounts on about 60 % of its offers | fake discount |
| D | a "Service fee" at checkout on every offer (€49 from €400, else €19) | hidden fee |
| E | "Only 2 left!" everywhere, all sales final, 41-day-old domain, not trusted | fake scarcity, no refund policy |

Totals with the current seed: 29 traps (3 fake discount, 8 hidden fee, 8 fake scarcity, 8 no
refund policy, 2 over budget) and 20 honest offers, which the eval uses to count honest deals
wrongly rejected.

## How this is checked

- **Now:** `pnpm exec vitest run scripts/gen-data` checks the data side of every row above:
  prices, checkout totals, price histories and the exact traps per demo offer (the `it.each` in
  `generate.test.ts` mirrors the demo table; change both together). It also checks that every
  trap in `traps.json` matches the data and cites records that exist.
- **Later:** whether Prickle actually *catches* each trap needs the checks that do not exist
  yet: `core/deal` (30-day rule) and `core/guard`, then the merchant-risk agent. `pnpm eval`
  runs every offer through Prickle and compares the outcomes with `traps.json`.
